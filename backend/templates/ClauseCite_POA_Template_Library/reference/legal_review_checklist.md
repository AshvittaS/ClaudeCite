# India / Tamil Nadu Legal Review Checklist

This checklist is a workflow aid, not a legal opinion. Verify each item against current primary sources and facts.

## Core questions for every instrument
- Is the principal an adult with legal capacity for this act, acting voluntarily and without coercion?
- Is the act delegable? Does law require personal appearance, a prescribed form, a particular signatory or direct consent?
- Is the agent eligible and adequately identified? Is there a conflict, self-dealing risk, or need for independent advice?
- Does the instrument clearly identify acts, assets, counterparties, limits, duration, reporting and termination?
- What stamp duty/adjudication applies under current Tamil Nadu law based on instrument type, relationship, purpose, execution/use and property facts? Never hardcode rates in a template.
- Is registration compulsory, optional, advisable or required by the intended transaction/receiving authority? Confirm current Registration Act rules, state amendments and Sub-Registrar practice.
- Are notarisation, consular authentication, apostille, translation, witnesses, photographs, biometric verification or identity documents required?
- Does the recipient (bank, tax portal, hospital, RTO, registry, company, counterparty) have a prescribed mandate or reject a general-form POA?
- How will the original be held, copies controlled, revocation communicated and receipt acknowledged?
- Are privacy/data-minimisation and secure storage controls in place?

## Tamil Nadu checks
- Consult the current Indian Stamp Act, 1899 as amended for Tamil Nadu, applicable schedules/rules and official registration/stamp guidance.
- Confirm stamp duty and registration fee with the Tamil Nadu Registration Department/Sub-Registrar for the exact instrument and transaction; seek adjudication where appropriate.
- For immovable property, verify title, encumbrances, property schedule, applicable registration procedure and whether the attorney may execute/present the relevant document.
- Check local body rules for cremation/burial and local transport authority rules for vehicle transactions.
- If executed outside India or used outside the place of execution, verify authentication, stamping/adjudication timelines, translation and acceptance requirements.

## Template-specific high-risk flags
- Limited/Special: precise transaction and scope; no implied powers.
- General: avoid undifferentiated blanket grants; list powers and exclusions.
- Continuing financial: do not assume US-style durability; check incapacity, death, agency termination and bank acceptance.
- Banking: bank-specific mandate and channel controls; no credential sharing.
- Property: POA is not a conveyance; sale deed/title transfer, stamp duty and registration require separate analysis.
- Tax: Income-tax Act eligibility, prescribed authorisation, portal and digital-signature requirements.
- Healthcare: distinguish information-sharing from consent authority; assess Indian advance directive and patient-consent law.
- Disposition of remains: local authority, police/inquest, public-health, facility, customary and family-dispute considerations.
- Vehicle/business: statutory forms, RTO/insurer processes, entity approvals and regulated activity rules.
- Revocation: inspect original instrument for agency coupled with interest, pending transactions, third-party reliance and registration/public notice.

## Primary-source starting points to verify
- India Code: Indian Contract Act, 1872; Power-of-Attorney Act, 1882; Registration Act, 1908; Transfer of Property Act, 1882; Income-tax Act, 1961; Motor Vehicles Act, 1988; Indian Stamp Act, 1899 and Tamil Nadu amendments.
- Tamil Nadu Registration Department: current stamp duty, registration fee, registration procedure and official circulars.
- Income Tax Department: current authorised representative provisions, prescribed forms and e-filing portal guidance.
- Ministry of Road Transport and Highways / Tamil Nadu Transport Department: vehicle transaction rules and forms.
- Current Supreme Court of India directions and applicable medical ethics/professional rules for advance medical directives and healthcare decisions.
- Local municipal/corporation authority and relevant facility for cremation/burial requirements.

## Required workflow outcome
Record for each generated document: sources checked, date checked, reviewing advocate, unresolved assumptions, recipient acceptance, stamp/registration determination, and final execution version.
