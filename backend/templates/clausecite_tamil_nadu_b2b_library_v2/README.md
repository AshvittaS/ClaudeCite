# ClauseCite Tamil Nadu B2B Legal Template Library — v2.0

60 standalone B2B legal drafting forms, each supplied as DOCX and structured JSON, across 12 categories.

## Tamil Nadu default
The default execution State is Tamil Nadu. The templates ask for the district/place of execution and keep State override configurable. No stamp-duty rate is hardcoded.

## Execution checks
Before execution, classify the instrument and verify the current stamp duty under the Indian Stamp Act, 1899 as applicable in Tamil Nadu, including State amendments and Schedule I. Confirm accepted stamping method and timing with current authority/counsel. Separately determine registration under the Registration Act, 1908 and applicable Tamil Nadu amendments/rules. Property, lease, security, mortgage, transfer and other registrable instruments require specialist review.

## What each form includes
- Parties, recitals and operative provisions
- Core commercial clauses
- Transaction-specific clauses
- Commercial particulars and payment schedules
- Transaction-specific annexure schedule
- Execution/signature blocks
- Tamil Nadu execution checklist
- Matching JSON inputs, ordered clauses, schedules and generation safeguards

## Important
These are detailed drafting starting points, not advocate-certified or universally execution-ready legal forms. An Indian advocate must review each form for the transaction, parties, current law, State-specific stamp duty, registration and regulatory facts. Required flags are workflow defaults and not legal conclusions.
