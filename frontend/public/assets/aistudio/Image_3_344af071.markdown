## Brand & Style

This design system channels an avant-garde digital agency aesthetic—combining ultra-dark minimalism with electric hyper-contrast. Targeted at tech visionaries, high-growth founders, and design-led enterprises, the interface conveys elite technical precision, creative confidence, and fluid sophistication.

The design movement bridges dark-mode minimalism, refined glassmorphism, and high-impact kinetic typography:
- **Atmosphere:** Pitch-black canvases layered over matte carbon structures create infinite depth without visual clutter.
- **Electric Accent:** Vibrant neon orange punctures the void, serving as a dynamic beacon for focal interactions, status anchors, and decisive calls-to-action.
- **Glass & Light:** Translucent dark membranes, soft glowing edge highlights, and subtle linear gradient strokes deliver an ethereal, tactile depth without heavy skeuomorphic physics.
- **Kinetic Precision:** Elements favor sleek, rounded silhouettes and pill containers, radiating an aerodynamic, polished product feel.

## Colors

The palette is tuned specifically for deep OLED immersion, anchored by pitch black (`#0a0a0c`) and carbon neutrals (`#121216`, `#19191f`), against which vibrant chromatic light and crisp whites take center stage.

- **Primary (`#ff5520`):** Kinetic neon orange used for primary CTAs, active states, active cursor blooms, and glowing ambient halos.
- **Secondary (`#ffffff`):** Pure optical white reserved for high-impact headlines, primary body content, and high-contrast badges.
- **Tertiary (`#ff8359`):** A lighter, luminous solar orange used for hover accents, gradient endpoints, and secondary focal highlights.
- **Neutral (`#0a0a0c`):** An uncompromising, pitch-black canvas establishing pure contrast and low-light visual tranquility.
- **Surface Tiers & Glow Accents:** `carbon-surface` (`#121216`) provides flat container differentiation, while `glass-border` (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`) and `glass-border-glow` (`rgba(255, 85, 32, 0.35)`) deliver microscopic razor-thin structural definition.

## Typography

The typographic strategy pairs the geometric, forward-leaning character of **Space Grotesk** with the balanced, ultra-legible ergonomics of **Plus Jakarta Sans**.

- **Display & Headlines:** Set in Space Grotesk with snug, negative tracking (-0.02em to -0.04em) to produce an authoritative, agency-grade editorial voice. Titles demand attention through tight line heights and high-contrast optical weights.
- **Body & Continuous Reading:** Handled by Plus Jakarta Sans. Its rounded geometry complements the display typography while keeping dense technical text, case studies, and descriptive agency copy effortless to scan.
- **Labels & Interactive Elements:** Rendered in Space Grotesk uppercase or medium weight with expanded tracking to maintain crisp edge contrast against dark glass surfaces and pill buttons.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout embraces a fluid, responsive 12-column grid system with generous vertical breathing room, framing interactive showcase modules like cinematic plates.

- **Breakpoints & Grids:**
  - **Desktop (≥ 1200px):** 12 columns, 5rem outer margins (`margin-lg`), and 2rem gutters (`gutter-lg`). Max content boundary capped at 1440px for ultra-wide setups.
  - **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8 columns, 3rem outer margins (`margin-md`), and 1.5rem gutters (`gutter`).
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, 1.5rem outer margins (`margin`), and 1rem gutters (`gutter-sm`).
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Sections deploy generous vertical paddings (4rem to 8rem) to create rhythmic pauses, focusing visual energy on featured interactive portfolios and agency artifacts.
- **Gap & Stacking Rules:** Nested component elements maintain a baseline 8px spatial modularity (`space-sm` for compact relationships, `space-md` for standard item groups, and `space-xl` between major modular zones).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not use murky drop shadows; instead, it utilizes translucent glassmorphism, carbon surface tiers, and razor-sharp glowing perimeter highlights.

- **Tonal Layers:**
  - **Base Canvas:** Deep pitch black (`#0a0a0c`), zero elevation.
  - **Level 1 (Panels & Groupings):** Solid carbon surface (`#121216`) with an ultra-fine border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`.
  - **Level 2 (Interactive Glass Surfaces):** Semi-transparent carbon (`rgba(18, 18, 22, 0.72)`) with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)` and perimeter stroke `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
  - **Level 3 (Modals & Overlays):** Translucent backdrop blur with deep ambient carbon tint (`rgba(25, 25, 31, 0.85)`).
- **The Outcrowd Glow:**
  - Interactive cards and floating panels feature directional gradient borders that highlight under cursor movement.
  - Hover states initiate a neon bloom: `box-shadow: 0 0 30px -5px rgba(255, 85, 32, 0.25), inset 0 1px 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a futuristic, aerodynamic shape language characterized by pill-shaped interactive anchors and rounded glass surfaces:

- **Buttons & Tags:** Strictly pill-shaped (`border-radius: 9999px` or level 3). They read instantly as tactile capsules floating effortlessly over the dark background.
- **Cards & Visual Containers:** Styled with generous corner rounding (`rounded-xl` / 24px - 32px), creating smooth pebble contours that soften dense technical layouts and media assets.
- **Input Fields & Segmented Controls:** Maintain the full pill aesthetic (`rounded-full`), reinforcing consistent touchpoints across forms and dynamic agency filters.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Full pill capsule (`border-radius: 9999px`), neon orange background (`#ff5520`), white typography (`label-lg`), with zero border. On hover, triggers a scale transform (1.02) and an ambient orange halo bloom (`box-shadow: 0 0 24px rgba(255, 85, 32, 0.45)`).
- **Secondary Glass:** Pill capsule, translucent carbon fill (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`), `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`, white text. On hover, border shifts to neon orange (`#ff5520`) with text color retained in pure white.
- **Icon / Action Orb:** Perfect circular buttons with micro-arrow glyphs, rotating 45 degrees upon hover.

### Cards & Showcase Panels
- **Glassmorphic Portfolio Card:** Background `rgba(18, 18, 22, 0.65)`, blurred backdrop (`16px`), rounded with 24px corners. Outlined by `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.07)`.
- **Active / Hover State:** Border transitions into an illuminated orange gradient with a radiant top-right edge highlight. Embedded media transitions with a smooth cinematic zoom (scale 1.04).

### Chips & Badges
- Pill-shaped tags with `body-sm` or `label-md` typography.
- Filled with low-opacity carbon (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`), subtle border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- Status indicators feature an 8px circular dot radiating an active neon orange pulse animation.

### Form Inputs
- Pill container with height 52px, filled with `#121216`, framed by `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`.
- Placeholder text in `text-muted` (`#8e8e99`).
- **Focus State:** Border transitions dynamically to `#ff5520`, accompanied by a subtle inner glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(255, 85, 32, 0.15)`).

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Radio elements use concentric pill/circle styling: inactive state has a dark `#121216` core with a `1px` subtle outline; active state deploys a radiant `#ff5520` center ring.
- Checkboxes feature a 6px curved corner box (`#121216`), flipping to full neon orange with an optical white check glyph upon selection.

### Lists & Project Indexes
- Clean horizontal rows separated by hairline dividers (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`).
- On row hover, initiates a horizontal reveal of category tags and an animated pill cursor preview with fluid vertical expansion.
